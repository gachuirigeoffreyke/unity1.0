## Loss caps

| Cap | KES | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Loss limit before demand is proven | 50,000 | A tenth of the budget |
| At risk in the holiday test | 30,000 | Leaves 20,000 for one more small test at Valentine's Day |
| Set-up costs that cannot be recovered | Up to 15,000 | Registration, permits, packaging, ads, samples |
| Non-returnable stock on hand | Up to 15,000 | Weekly batches; sale-or-return preferred |

> Break-even Boxes to break even = set-up costs ÷ margin per box. At KES 15,000 and a KES 500 margin, that is 30 boxes; at a KES 1,000 margin, 15 boxes. The model reruns this once the makers' quotes are in. Money controls All sales go into the till; all spending is logged the same day. Spending above an agreed limit needs both founders (Volume 1). Money at risk is checked against the caps every week. Loan repayments are scheduled in the model before any loan is drawn. Tax at a glance (to confirm in R9.1) The business name is taxed through the partner's own KRA PIN. Turnover tax covers turnover of KES 1–25 million; sources disagree on the rate (1.5% or 3%), so the model uses 3% until it is confirmed. VAT registration is compulsory above KES 5 million of taxable turnover; KRA proposed removing that threshold in March 2026. The company is taxed separately from 2027.
