# Planties · Volume 7 · Operations & Fulfilment

## Summary

Delivery from the farm to Nairobi is the biggest operating-cost risk. Fixed delivery days by zone and a minimum order value keep it under control.

## Order flow

1. Order arrives by WhatsApp or DM (web store later).
2. Confirm stock, price, delivery zone and day; take payment by M-Pesa.
3. Pick, pot, clean and pack at the farm.
4. Batch orders by zone and dispatch on that zone's delivery day.
5. Deliver with a care card; follow up with a message after seven days.
6. Log the sale, delivery cost and feedback.
## Delivery options to compare (research R6)

| Option | Best for | Watch-outs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Motorbike courier per order | Single urgent orders | Cost per order; plant damage |
| Batched delivery days by zone | Most home orders | Customer waits for the zone day |
| Nairobi pickup point | Low-value orders | Needs a partner location |
| Own vehicle | Volume, events, landscaping | Capex; Phase 3 |

## Packing standard

Pot secured, soil covered, leaves protected. Tested on real deliveries during the Nov–Dec holiday market test before launch.
