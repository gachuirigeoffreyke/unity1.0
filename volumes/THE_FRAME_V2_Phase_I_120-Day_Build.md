# THE FRAME Playbook · V2 · Phase I · The 120-Day Build

**Phase I runs 17 weeks, from Sat 31 Oct 2026 to Gate 1 on Sat 27 Feb 2027. Its job is to leave you slightly bigger and definitely sharper.**

- You train six days a week at home, in whichever column you can set up.
- You test four times along the way.
- It finishes with a four-week mini-cut and a peak week.

Everything in this volume is the v1.1 programme, restructured; nothing in it has changed. Version 2.1 (29 Oct 2026) moved only the dates: Day 1 is now Sat 31 Oct 2026 and weeks run Saturday to Friday.

## 3. Running a session

**Every lifting session follows the same routine and takes 60–75 minutes.**

1. **Warm up.** 2–3 minutes of marching or skipping, then one or two lighter sets of the first move.
2. **Work down the table.** Paired rows (A1 and A2, B1 and B2, and so on) alternate as supersets.
3. **Pick a column for each move.** Use the hardest version you can set up today. Mixing columns in one session is fine; sets, reps and RIR stay the same.
4. **Rest.** 2–3 minutes on big moves, 60–90 seconds on small ones.
5. **Respect the pain rules.**
   - Knee pain up to 3/10 during a set is fine if it's gone by the next morning.
   - Sharp, catching or shooting back pain ends that exercise for the day.
6. **Log every working set.** Record the move, column, variation or load, reps, RIR and any pain score on the Log sheet.

**Effort by block.**

- **Block 1:** RIR 2–3, with RIR 3 in Week 1 while you learn the moves.
- **Blocks 2–3:** RIR 1–2.
- **Always:** hinges, split squats, and anything that loads the knee or back stay at RIR 2 or more.
- **Light columns:** in the Bodyweight and Intermediary columns, take safe moves closer to failure, because light loads need it (V1, Law 1).

## 6. How to do the less familiar moves

The workbook's Library sheet has the setup, cues and watch-points for every move. These are the ones people most often get stuck on.

- **Back widow.** Lie on your back, elbows bent by your sides. Drive the elbows into the floor to lift your upper back, and hold 2 s.
- **One-arm door-frame row.** Grip the frame with one hand, feet close to it. Lean back with the arm straight, then pull yourself in. Wear shoes on smooth floors.
- **Door-frame curl.** Hold the frame palm up, lean back with the arm straight, and curl yourself in with the biceps.
- **Towel or bedsheet door row.** Knot the towel or sheet and shut the knot in the top or side of a solid door that opens away from you. Lock the door, test it with a hard tug, then lean back and row your chest to the door.
- **Jerrycan or backpack floor pullover.** On your back, lower the load behind your head with nearly straight arms, then pull it back over your chest.
- **Wall-press lateral hold.** Stand side-on to a wall and press the back of the wrist outward into it, hard, for 10 s.
- **Table triceps extension.** With your hands on a table edge and your body straight in a lean, bend only the elbows to lower your head below the edge, then press back.
- **Spanish squat.**
  - Twist a bedsheet into a rope, or use a heavy band.
  - Loop it round a pillar or gate post at knee height and behind both knees.
  - Sit back with shins vertical and hold. Test the anchor first.
- **Split squat to a cushion.** Lower straight down until the back knee touches a cushion, then drive up through the front heel. The cushion's height sets your depth (section 8).
- **Single-leg RDL.** Stand on one leg with the knee soft. Hinge back so your torso and free leg stay in one line, reach for the floor, and return. Keep a wall nearby for balance.
- **Bridge walkout.** Hold a glute bridge and walk the heels out and back in small steps without the hips dropping.
- **Big 3.** Curl-up (lift head and shoulders 2–3 cm and hold 10 s), side plank, and bird dog (reach opposite arm and leg and hold 10 s). Do 6, then 4, then 2 holds of each.
- **Inner-tube band.** An old bicycle inner tube from a bike repair shop works as a band; knot it to length and replace it if it cracks.
- **Prone Y-T-W raise.** Lie face down with your forehead on a towel. Lift straight arms into a Y, then a T, then a W, squeezing the shoulder blades each time.

## 8. Knee and back during Phase I

**Both get loaded on purpose, at a dose set by the pain rules.** V5 holds the full protocols; these are the Phase I specifics.

**Knee.**

- **Primer first.** Every lower session starts with the knee primer: a wall sit or Spanish squat held at a 30–60° bend.
- **Split squat depth.**
  - Start at the cushion height that keeps pain at 3/10 or less.
  - After a week where every session stayed at 3/10 or less and was no worse the next morning, lower the cushion one step.
  - Full depth comes when the knee says so, not on a date.
- **Step-ups** start at 20 cm and rise to 30 and 40 cm as the knee allows.
- **Reverse lunges** join Lower B in Week 7, and only if split squats are pain-free at full depth.
- **Outside training:** keep the car seat and desk fixes from v1.1. The clutch should go to the floor with your knee still slightly bent, and your knees shouldn't stay cramped under the desk.

**Back.**

- **Block 1:** the hip-hinge drill, single-leg RDLs, the Big 3 and light carries.
- **From Week 7:** jerrycan RDLs, starting at 2 × 10 L and adding 2–5 L only when every set is clean at RIR 2.
- **Twinge:** any sharp or shooting pain ends that move for the day (section 12 covers the next 48 hours).
- **Red flags:** pain or tingling below the knee, numbness, leg weakness or night pain means a doctor or physio. Groin numbness or a new bladder or bowel change means emergency care.

## 9. Fuel during Phase I

**Your Day-1 waist sets your lane, and the weekly check-in steers it.** V6 has the full system.

| Day-1 waist at the navel | Lane | Start calories |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 81 cm or less | A · Lean gain | About 2,800 kcal |
| 82–86 cm | B · Recomp (default until measured) | About 2,600 kcal |
| 87 cm or more | C · Lean-out | About 2,250 kcal |

- **Protein:** about 150 g a day (never below 120 g), in four feedings of 30–41 g.
- **Adjustments:** calories change by 150–200 kcal only after two weeks outside your lane's band.
- **Lane C:** holds maintenance calories over Weeks 12–13 (the second deload and the week after), a short diet break before the mini-cut.
- **Mini-cut:** every lane drops about 300 kcal for Weeks 14–17.
- **Holidays:** Christmas and New Year fall in Weeks 8–9, on Engine and Reset days, so no lifting session moves. Eat at your lane's calories and keep the protein. One or two festive meals don't need making up.

## 10. The test battery

**Four Saturdays, about 20 minutes each:** Day 1 (31 Oct), Day 43 (12 Dec), Day 85 (23 Jan) and Day 120 (27 Feb). The record grid is on W2; the Tests sheet in the workbook holds the same numbers.

**How to keep the tests fair.**

- **Same conditions.** Test at the same time of day, before any other training, after a 5-minute warm-up.
- **Same order every time.** Knee checks, push-ups, pulling test, split squats (left then right), bridges (left then right), side planks (left then right).
- **Rest and stop rules.** Rest 2–3 minutes between tests, and stop any test that takes pain above 3/10.
- **Measurements and photos.** Measure waist, arm and chest before breakfast. Take photos in the same light and spot.
- **Pulling test.** If you have no bar yet, use table rows with legs straight. Then keep that same test for all four days, so the numbers compare.

| Test | How |
| --- | --- |
| Push-ups | Max clean reps, chest to a fist's height, no sag |
| Pulling test | Pull-ups from a dead hang; with no bar, table rows with legs straight |
| Split squat, left and right | Max reps to your cushion depth, 2-s lowering |
| Single-leg bridge, left and right | Max reps, 1-s squeeze at the top |
| Side plank, left and right | Seconds, until the hips drop |
| Knee pain on stairs | 0–10, one flight up and down |
| Knee pain after sitting | 0–10, on standing after 60 minutes seated |
| Weight | 7-day average from the Daily sheet |
| Waist, arm, chest | cm, relaxed, same spot each time |
| Photos | Front, side and back |

**Reading the results.**

- **Each retest should move.** Most tests should show more reps or a harder rung, left-right gaps should shrink, and knee scores should fall.
- **If results are flat two retests running,** check sleep, food and adherence first. Only then change the plan, through the gate rules.

## 11. Gate 1 · Day 120

**On Sat 27 Feb 2027 you turn Phase I's data into three decisions.** The Gate 1 box on W2 is the checklist you fill in on the day.

**Inputs:**

- Day-120 test battery
- 7-day average weight in Week 17
- Waist on Day 120 against Day 1
- Sessions done in Weeks 13–17, out of 30
- Highest knee pain in the last four weeks
- Back episodes during Phase I
- Kit owned
- Study crunch dates for the next 18 weeks

**Decision 1 · Gain lane and surplus.**

| If | Then |
| --- | --- |
| Waist 81 cm or less and 24+ sessions | Gain at 0.25–0.5% of bodyweight a week, starting at 0.25% |
| Waist 82–86 cm and 24+ sessions | Gain at 0.25% a week; the waist may rise 0.5 cm per two weeks at most |
| Waist 87 cm or more, or under 24 sessions | Recomp at maintenance for the first 6-week cycle; recheck on Day 176 |
| Knee above 3/10 on stairs, or a back episode in the last 4 weeks | Run the V5 protocol before adding leg load |
| Data missing | Gain at 0.25% a week |

**Decision 2 · Home kit or gym.** Choose the gym if two of these are true:

- Your leg loads stalled from Day 85 to Day 120 at your top home load.
- You can commit to 3 or more gym sessions a week, including travel.
- The fee fits the budget.

Otherwise, stay with the home kit and consider the jua kali station in V9. If data is missing, the default is the home kit.

**Decision 3 · Phase II split.** Keep the four-day upper/lower split. Add a 30-minute shoulders, arms and upper-back session on one Engine day only if you did 27 or more sessions in Weeks 13–17 and averaged 7.5 hours of sleep or more. If data is missing, the default is to keep the four-day split.

Record the three choices on W2. Then start the Reset phase (Weeks 18–19, at maintenance calories); V3 takes over from there.

## 12. When things go wrong

- **Missed session.** Shift the week forward. Never stack two sessions in a day, and never miss two lifting days running.
- **Bad night (under 6 hours).** Drop one set per exercise and add one RIR for that session.
- **Illness.** No training with a fever or chest symptoms. Come back at half the sets and RIR 3, and build back over a week (V4 and V7 have the details).
- **Travel or a crunch week.** Hold the maintenance minimum: one hard session a week, one set per exercise, same effort, in whatever column you can set up.
- **Knee flare.**
  - Pain above 3/10, or worse the next morning: shorten the range or cut the load next session.
  - Three flares in a row: switch to the V5 protocol.
  - Swelling, locking or giving way: see a physiotherapist.
- **Back twinge.**
  - Stop hinges and carries for 48 hours, but keep walking and doing the Big 3.
  - Return one rung lower than before.
  - Red flags mean a doctor; emergency signs mean emergency care.
