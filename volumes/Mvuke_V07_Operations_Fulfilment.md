# Mvuke · Volume 7 · Operations & Fulfilment

## Summary

One site in Kiambu, sealed stock only, written disclosure before every order and confirmation after it, courier or boda delivery to the Nairobi suburbs, and a clear refund policy.

## Order to delivery

1. A buyer enquires through Instagram, WhatsApp, a Planties referral code, a pop-up or a corporate contact.
2. Send the disclosure: business name, contacts, contents, price including delivery, delivery time and the refund policy.
3. The buyer pays to the M-Pesa till.
4. Send a written order confirmation.
5. Pick stock in expiry order, check labels and expiry dates, and pack to the packing checklist (R7.4); add a safety card where an oil is included.
6. Book the courier or rider, and photograph the box at dispatch.
7. Confirm delivery, then send the survey.
8. Log the order in the Pilot Test Log.
## Storage

One site in Kiambu, with the partner. Cool, dry and shaded, on shelves off the floor, away from fuel, chemicals and pests. At least 6 months' shelf life left at sale; first to expire, first out. Non-returnable stock on hand stays under KES 15,000; buy in weekly batches against orders.

## Payments

An M-Pesa till in the business name, opened as soon as the certificate arrives. Until then, a separate line used only for sales. No personal numbers.

> Delivery Courier or boda rider from the Kiambu site to the Nairobi suburbs, at standard zone fees paid by the buyer. Zones and fees come from R7.3 and go on the model's Delivery sheet. One dry-run delivery per zone before 16 November.
