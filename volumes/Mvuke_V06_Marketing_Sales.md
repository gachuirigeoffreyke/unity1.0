# Mvuke · Volume 6 · Marketing & Sales

## Summary

Sell through Instagram, WhatsApp, Planties' referrals, pop-ups and corporate hampers; talk about taste, aroma, origin and ritual, never about curing anything.

## Brand

Mvuke means steam in Kiswahili: rising from a cup or a bath. A light web search found no Kenyan brand using it. Formal checks are still needed: BRS, KIPI (classes 3, 30 and 35), the .co.ke domain, and Instagram and TikTok handles (R8.1). Names already taken: Jani (a Limuru beauty brand) and Mizizi (a Sheth Naturals range). A name idea for the holding company: Udongo, meaning soil. Open.

## Wording rules

These apply to the founders, to any maker content Mvuke reposts, and to the herbalist network.

Say: taste, aroma, origin, ingredients, how to brew or use, the ritual, the gift, the grower's story. Never say: treats, cures, heals, prevents, detox, boosts immunity, fights, anti- inflammatory, balances hormones, lowers blood pressure or sugar, fertility, weight loss, safe in pregnancy, no side effects, doctor-approved. When a buyer asks about a health condition: we can't advise on health conditions or medicines; please ask your doctor or pharmacist before use if you're pregnant, breastfeeding, on medication or have a condition. No reposting of posts or testimonials that make health claims. R3.4 and R8.4 confirm the exact limits.

## Planties referral

5% of the box price on sales that Planties' channels refer, tracked by a code or link and paid monthly. Plants inside boxes are bought from Planties at an agreed wholesale price. Written down and approved by the Planties partner, because Mjenzi sits on both sides; reviewed after the test. Benchmark: platform affiliate rates run 2–11% at Jumia and 2–8% at Kilimall.
