## How this playbook works

This playbook is the one place where Planties' decisions and operating steps live. Research answers questions, the answers become decisions here, and decisions that work in practice become runbooks.

1. Research. Questions are answered in the Planties Research Playbook , and each finding is tagged with the volume it feeds.
2. Decide. Findings turn into decisions inside the volumes, and each one is recorded in the Decisions log below.
3. Run. Once a process has worked in practice, its steps move to Volume 11 as a checklist anyone in the business can follow.

The numbers live in the separate Planties financial model workbook (Excel). Amounts are in Kenya shillings (KES). Planties is the working name until the brand is settled.

Mjenzi maintains the playbook. The full-time partner reviews the production and operations volumes.
