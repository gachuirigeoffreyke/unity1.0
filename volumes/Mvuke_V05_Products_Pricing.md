## Pricing rules

Margin per box = price − goods − packaging − delivery cost not recovered − M-Pesa fees − Planties commission. Floor: at least KES 500 per box. Below that, raise the price or change the contents before buying. Break-even = set-up costs ÷ margin per box. At KES 15,000 of set-up costs and KES 500 a box, that is 30 boxes, the stop line. Buyers pay standard zone delivery fees (Volume 7). Prices are set from the R2 price scan and the makers' quotes.

> Essential oils (test rules) Only from KEBS-certified makers, preferably as diluted roll-ons or mists. No wintergreen or camphor, and none in baby, children's or pregnancy gifts. Every oil ships with a safety card: external use only; never swallow; dilute and patch test; keep away from eyes, children and pets; ask a doctor first if pregnant, breastfeeding or on medication. A pharmacist reviews the card (R13).
