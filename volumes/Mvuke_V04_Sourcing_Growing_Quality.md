# Mvuke · Volume 4 · Sourcing, Growing & Quality

## Summary

Buy only sealed, certified products from licensed makers, each with a complete supplier file; white-label next; grow a few herbs from year 1 once water and land terms are settled.

## Choosing makers

A maker qualifies when:

each product carries a valid KEBS permit, checked by SMS to 20023, and a complete label with no medical claims; the wholesale price leaves the KES 500 margin floor per box (Volume 5); it accepts small orders, and ideally sale-or-return or consignment; its packaging is glass, paper or tin rather than plastic, where possible; it states fair sourcing (organic certification is a plus); it can supply in December and, ideally, carries product liability insurance.

## Supplier file (one per product)

| Field | What to keep |
| --- | --- |
| Maker | Name, location, phone, payment details |
| KEBS permit | Permit number, and the date and result of the SMS check |
| Invoice | Price, quantity, date |
| Batch and expiry | Batch code and expiry date; at least 6 months left at sale |
| Label | Photos of the front and back |
| Terms | Minimum order, sale-or-return, lead time |

## White-label path (Phase 1)

1. Pick 2–3 products from the test's best sellers (R4.5).
2. Ask 3 makers for samples, minimums, unit costs and lead times (R5.2).
3. Confirm who holds the KEBS permit for a product under Mvuke's brand (R3.5).
4. A pharmacist reviews the ingredients and the safety wording (R4.2).
5. Design labels and plastic-free packaging (R7.5).
6. Certify, then launch for Mother's Day, 9 May 2027.
## Growing (from year 1)

Start small on family land near Limuru–Kiambu, in a permaculture mix of herbs, flowers and food crops run for business output. Water first: measure what is available on both pieces of land before planting (R6.2). Land terms first too: a title search and a written licence on each family's land, as in the Planties playbook's Volume 3 (R6.5). Both families get a full proposal, not only the money side. Plant ahead of the long rains, March to May 2027. Drying needs a clean, dry set-up, and selling own-grown dried herbs may trigger certification (R6.4, R6.7).

## Organic and plastic-free

Organic in practice, honestly described. The word organic stays off labels until certified; group and third-party certification are compared in R6.6. Plastic-free applies to Mvuke's outer box, fill and wrap now, and to primary packaging once Mvuke has its own products. No repacking of makers' products.

> Working with the herbalist network Knowledge is used only with consent, credit and fair payment; the traditional- knowledge rules are confirmed in R12.1. Raw herbs only: their finished remedies are never resold. They do not speak for Mvuke, and Mvuke does not promote their remedies. Quality basics Every item has at least 6 months' shelf life left at sale; stock is used in expiry order. Storage is cool, dry, off the floor, and away from fuel, chemicals and pests. Own products will need testing before launch (R4.3).
